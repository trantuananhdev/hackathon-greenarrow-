Điện Biên has complex terrain with fast-changing, extreme weather — dense fog, sudden floods, and winter frost that seriously affect agriculture, transport, and highland residents' lives. Forecast information currently exists at the provincial level, but commune/village residents receive it late, it isn't specific enough for each sub-region, and it's presented in ways that are hard to understand for people who don't read text well. Problem to solve — Build an AI solution delivering weather information to the right people, at the right time, in the right language:

Sub-regional detailed forecasts: Process weather data to produce forecasts more specific than the provincial level — by district or commune cluster, especially in disaster-prone areas. Early warnings with concrete actions: When a risk is detected (heavy rain, frost, flash flood), the AI automatically generates a simple warning bulletin with recommended actions for residents and village officials. Multi-channel and multilingual: Distribute information via Zalo, SMS, or public loudspeakers — with the ability to convert to local ethnic languages (Thái, Mông/Hmong) for ethnic-minority areas. Intuitive interface for residents: Use icons, colors, and a simple warning scale instead of technical text.

Data / Resources:
Weather data APIs: Open-Meteo, OpenWeatherMap (free, high-resolution), plus data from the Điện Biên Hydro-Meteorological Station. Historical disaster and warning data for Điện Biên from the Steering Committee for Natural Disaster Prevention & Search and Rescue.

Minimum submission requirements:
Forecast demo: display a 3–7 day forecast for at least 3 different locations in Điện Biên. Warning mechanism: automatically issue a warning when a pre-defined dangerous-weather threshold is exceeded. Simple interface: residents who can't read a meteorological forecast still understand the danger level. Architecture document + 1-page deck: source data, processing model, distribution channels, deployment roadmap.
